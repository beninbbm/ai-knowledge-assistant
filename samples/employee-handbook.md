# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may organise your day as you see fit, provided you work an average of 37.5 hours per week. Please keep your calendar up to date so colleagues know when you are available.

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

### Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Contractors are not eligible for paid parental leave, but may agree unpaid time off with their engagement manager.

### Sick Leave

You may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year without a doctor's note. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, please provide a note to People Ops.

## Remote Work

Northwind Labs is remote-first. You may work from any country where we have a legal entity for up to 60 days per year without prior approval. Working from other countries requires approval from People Ops at least 4 weeks in advance.

- A one-off home office budget of $1,200 is available in your first 90 days.
- Internet costs are reimbursed up to $50 per month.
- Co-working memberships are reimbursed up to $250 per month with manager approval.

## Code of Conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, People Ops, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.
