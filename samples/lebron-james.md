# LeBron James: Player Profile

## Overview

LeBron Raymone James is an American professional basketball player, born on 30 December 1984 in Akron, Ohio. Nicknamed "King James", he stands 6 ft 9 in (2.06 m) and plays mainly as a forward. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest basketball players of all time.

## Early Life

James was raised in Akron by his mother, Gloria. He attended St. Vincent-St. Mary High School, where his games drew national television coverage and he appeared on the cover of Sports Illustrated as a junior. He entered the NBA straight from high school.

## NBA Career

### Cleveland Cavaliers (2003-2010)

The Cleveland Cavaliers selected James with the first overall pick in the 2003 NBA draft. He won Rookie of the Year for the 2003-04 season and the league's Most Valuable Player award in 2009 and 2010. He led Cleveland to the 2007 NBA Finals, where they lost to the San Antonio Spurs.

### Miami Heat (2010-2014)

In 2010 James joined the Miami Heat, announcing the move in a televised special known as "The Decision". Alongside Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh he reached four straight NBA Finals and won championships in 2012 and 2013, earning the MVP and Finals MVP awards in both seasons.

### Return to Cleveland (2014-2018)

James returned to the Cavaliers in 2014. In the 2016 NBA Finals, Cleveland came back from a 3-1 series deficit against the Golden State Warriors to win the city's first major professional sports title in 52 years. James was named Finals MVP.

### Los Angeles Lakers (2018-present)

James signed with the Los Angeles Lakers in 2018. He won his fourth championship and fourth Finals MVP in 2020, in a season completed in a protected "bubble" near Orlando, Florida. On 7 February 2023 he passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar to become the NBA's all-time leading scorer, and in March 2024 he became the first player to score 40,000 career points. In October 2024 he and his son Bronny became the first father and son to play in an NBA game together.

## Finances

James is one of the wealthiest athletes in history. In 2022 Forbes reported that he had become the first active NBA player to be worth more than $1 billion. Most of his fortune comes from endorsements and business investments rather than his playing salary. Figures below are publicly reported estimates.

### NBA Salary

James has earned more in NBA salary than any other player in league history. In July 2024 he signed a two-year contract with the Lakers reportedly worth about $101 million, slightly below the maximum he was eligible for, which helped the team stay under salary limits.

### Endorsements

Before playing an NBA game, James signed a sneaker deal with Nike in 2003 reported at around $90 million over seven years. In 2015 he signed a lifetime contract with Nike, reportedly the first of its kind for the company, with a value widely estimated at more than $1 billion. He has also endorsed brands such as Coca-Cola (Sprite), Beats by Dre, AT&T and PepsiCo.

### Philanthropy

In 2015 his foundation and the University of Akron announced that qualifying students in its programmes could receive four-year scholarships, a commitment reported to be worth more than $41 million. The I PROMISE School is funded jointly by the foundation and Akron Public Schools.

## Off the Court

Through the LeBron James Family Foundation, James opened the I PROMISE School in Akron in 2018, a public school for at-risk children that also supports their families. He co-founded the media company SpringHill and has appeared in films including Trainwreck (2015) and Space Jam: A New Legacy (2021).

James married his high-school sweetheart, Savannah Brinson, in 2013. They have three children: LeBron Jr. (Bronny), Bryce and Zhuri.
